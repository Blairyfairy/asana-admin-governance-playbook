## Monthly
- [ ] **1. Audit licenses.** Pull membership reports; remove or downgrade inactive and non-contributing users; move stakeholders to view only licenses.
- [ ] **4. Vet integrations.** Review app approval requests, blocked/allowed apps, service accounts, and Personal Access Token policies.

## At each engagement close
- [ ] **2. Review guests and access.** Offboard guests and contractors; reassign their tasks; confirm trusted guest domains.

## Quarterly
- [ ] **3. Verify privacy and roles.** Check project and team privacy defaults; review RAC roles; confirm work access mode on sensitive projects.
- [ ] **5. Review Asana AI and AI Studio.** Confirm providers, web access, member access, and credit consumption.
- [ ] **6. Do a security checkup.** Access, authentication, configuration, data protection, patching, documentation; refresh the breach response plan.
- [ ] **7. Monitor and report.** Review the Insights tab; update governance and data-retention notes; refresh the onboarding template.
