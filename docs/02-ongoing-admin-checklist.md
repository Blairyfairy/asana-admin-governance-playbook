# Step 2: "Ongoing Admin Best Practices" Checklist

Seven items, each tied to a topic in Step 1 and a section of the Asana admin best practices PDF.

| # | Checklist item | Cadence | Topic | PDF section |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Audit licenses.** Pull membership reports, remove or downgrade inactive and non-contributing users, move stakeholders to view only. | Monthly | 1 | Manage your users; Security and billing |
| 2 | **Review guests and access.** Confirm trusted guest domains, review guest accounts at each engagement close, deprovision using the standard steps. | At engagement close + quarterly | 2 | Manage your users; Set up your environment |
| 3 | **Verify privacy and roles.** Check project and team privacy defaults, review RAC roles, use work access mode for sensitive projects. | Quarterly | 2 | Manage your users; Governance |
| 4 | **Vet integrations.** Review the app approval queue, blocked/allowed lists, service accounts, and Personal Access Token policies. | Monthly | 3 | AI and integrations; Apps and access |
| 5 | **Review Asana AI and AI Studio settings.** Confirm providers, web access, member access, and credit consumption. | Quarterly | 3 | AI and integrations |
| 6 | **Do a security checkup.** Cover authentication (SSO/2FA, password strength), configuration, data protection, documentation. Keep the breach response plan current. | Quarterly | 3 | Security and billing |
| 7 | **Monitor and report.** Check Insights data, update governance and data-retention notes, refresh the onboarding template. | Quarterly | All | Share data and insights; Governance |

A printable version is in `checklists/ongoing-admin-best-practices.md`.
